# Ghost Ward Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: 26 November 2026

App: Ghost Ward (Android, package com.thelightcome.ghostward)

Developer: TheLightCome

Contact: nurikserikbay1995@gmail.com


## Summary

Ghost Ward is an augmented reality horror game: you look around your room through the phone camera and investigate supernatural entities that appear in it. The game has no accounts, no chat and no sign-in, and it does not ask for your name or contacts. The camera picture and the microphone level are processed on your device only and are never recorded or sent to us. Your game progress stays on your device. The free game shows ads from Google AdMob; where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first.


## Camera and microphone

The game needs the camera to show your room and place entities in it. Camera frames are analysed on your device only, by Google Play Services for AR (ARCore) where it is available, or by a depth estimation model built into the game. The game does not record, store or upload camera images or video.

The game uses the microphone to measure how loud your surroundings are, so that entities can react to noise. Only the sound level is calculated, on your device. Audio is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

You can refuse or withdraw either permission in Android settings. Without the camera the game cannot show your room; without the microphone, sound-based events are turned off.


## Photos you take and share

When you take an in-game photo and press Share, the game saves that single screenshot to your device's photo gallery and opens the Android share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share it with, and that app's own policy then applies.


## Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only:

- the entities you have identified, your journal, trophies and best survival time;
- settings such as language, camera view, sound volumes, microphone sensitivity, vibration, graphics quality and AR mode;
- which camera views you have unlocked;
- how often ads have been shown, so they are not shown too often;
- your advertising consent choice, saved by Google's consent tool.
The developer does not run servers for this game and does not receive a copy of this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. Android may include it in your own device backup, depending on your settings.


## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: rewarded ads that you choose to watch for in-game rewards, and an occasional full-screen ad, at most after every third retry and no more than once every two minutes. Ads are limited to content rated T (Teen) or lower.

To deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects:

- the Android Advertising ID and an app-specific identifier (App Set ID);
- your IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location;
- interactions such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information such as app launch time and crashes;
- basic device information such as model and operating system version.
This information is encrypted in transit and is processed by Google under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings, Privacy. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings, under Google, Ads.


## Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy


## Game engine

Ghost Ward is built with the Unity engine. Unity may collect limited technical information about the device and app performance as described in Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy


## Children

Ghost Ward is a horror game for teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the game, write to us and we will explain what is stored on the device and help you contact Google where needed.


## Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with anyone except Google (AdMob and ARCore) and Unity, as described above, to make the game and its ads work.


## Legal basis (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, data is processed on these grounds: your consent for personalised ads and for the use of device identifiers for advertising; our legitimate interest in showing non-personalised ads that keep the game free, if you do not give consent; and our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and secure. Camera and microphone data never leave your device.


## International transfers

Google and Unity may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. They state that they protect such transfers with recognised safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.


## Security and retention

We do not run servers and keep no copy of your data. Game progress stays in the game's private storage on your device until you delete it. Information processed by Google or Unity is kept according to their own retention rules.


## Your rights and choices

- Ad consent: change it at any time in Settings, Privacy.
- Camera and microphone: allow or deny them in Android Settings, Apps, Ghost Ward, Permissions.
- Delete your data: uninstall the game or open Android Settings, Apps, Ghost Ward, Storage, Clear data.
- Your rights: depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data protection authority.
- Questions: write to us at the email below. We aim to reply within 30 days.

## Changes to this policy

If the game's features or the services it uses change, we will update this policy and the "last updated" date above. The current version is always available at the same address.
