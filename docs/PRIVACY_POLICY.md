# Ghost Ward — Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

Ghost Ward ("the app") is a mobile game published by TheLightCome ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have.

Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]

## 1. Camera

The game shows your room through the camera and places supernatural entities in it. Camera frames are processed **only on your device** (augmented reality and on-device depth estimation). We do not record, store or send camera images or video anywhere.

If you press "Photo" and then "Share", the app saves that one screenshot to your device's photo gallery and opens the system share sheet. Where the picture goes after that is your choice.

## 2. Microphone

The game listens to how loud your surroundings are so entities can react to noise. Only the sound level is measured, **on your device**. Audio is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

## 3. Game data

Progress, settings and the in-game journal are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. We have no servers and no user accounts.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional interstitial ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

- the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
- IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and OS version;
- information about ad views and clicks.

Ads are limited to content rated for teens (T) or lower.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Unity engine

The app is built with Unity. The engine may collect basic technical data (device model, OS, crash information) for stability purposes: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

## 7. Children

Ghost Ward is a horror game intended for teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with anyone except as described above (Google AdMob, Google ARCore, Unity), which process it under their own privacy policies.

## 9. Data deletion

We do not keep any of your data on our side. Uninstalling the app removes all game data from your device. For data collected by Google for advertising, use your Google account and Android ad settings.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
